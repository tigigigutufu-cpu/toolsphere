About this tool

This EMI calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula banks use for home, car and personal loans: EMI = P×r×(1+r)n / ((1+r)n−1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Frequently asked questions

Is this EMI calculation accurate?

It uses the same reducing-balance formula banks use, but your actual EMI may vary slightly based on processing fees and the exact disbursement date.

Does this tool store my loan data?

No, everything is calculated locally in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
